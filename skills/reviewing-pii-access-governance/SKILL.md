# Reviewing PII Access Governance

Use this when models touch PII, regulated data, or access policy.

Checklist:

- Identify sensitive columns and required masking, hashing, tokenization, or access controls.
- Confirm downstream consumers and environment promotion are approved for the data class.
- Require audits/tests that prevent accidental exposure or grain expansion.
- Add redaction notes before publishing Jira or Confluence artifacts.
